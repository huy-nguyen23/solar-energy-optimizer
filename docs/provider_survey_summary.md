# Provider Survey Summary

Below is a summary of the answers collected from the real-world survey:

**Group 1: Practical System Consulting**
- **Question 1:** For a 2 million VND/month bill (equivalent to 700 - 770 kWh/month), providers typically recommend a **5 kWp** or **6 kWp** system (offsetting 80-90% of demand).
- **Questions 2, 3, 4:** 
  - **3 kWp:** 350-400 kWh/month, 15-18 m², Cost: 40-48 million (Grid-tied) / 75-90 million (Hybrid).
  - **5 kWp:** 600-650 kWh/month, 25-30 m², Cost: 60-70 million (Grid-tied) / 110-130 million (Hybrid).
  - **6 kWp:** 720-800 kWh/month, 30-36 m², Cost: 72-85 million (Grid-tied) / 130-150 million (Hybrid).
  - **10 kWp:** 1200-1300 kWh/month, 50-60 m², Cost: 110-130 million (Grid-tied) / 200-230 million (Hybrid).
- **Question 5:** Payback period: **Grid-tied (3-5 years)**, **Hybrid (4-6 years)**.

**Group 2: Technical Specifications**
- **Questions 6 & 7:** Popular Tier 1 brands: Jinko, Longi, Canadian. Common capacities: **550Wp - 620Wp**.
- **Question 8:** Panel efficiency: **21.5% - 23.2%**.
- **Question 9:** Popular inverters: Huawei, Sungrow (Premium) or Deye, Luxpower (Mainstream Hybrid). Max efficiency: **97.5% - 98.6%**.
- **Question 10:** Total system losses: **18% - 22%**. Safe design PR is typically assumed to be **78% - 80%**.

**Group 3: Real-World Influencing Factors**
- **Question 11:** Shading reduces output by **20-80%** (if covering 1 string). West/East facing reduces output by 5-12%, North facing reduces by 15-25%. Dust reduces output by 5-15%. The combined loss from these factors is conservatively around **10%**.
- **Question 12:** If consuming mostly at night, it is **Highly Recommended** to use a Hybrid system to discharge the battery at night.
- **Question 13:** Lithium battery storage increases system costs by an additional **50% - 80%**.
- **Question 14:** 5 core details to collect: Electricity bill amount, Consumption habits (day/night), Roof structure, Geographical area, and Primary goal.
- **Question 15:** Real-world Case Study: A 5.5kWp Hybrid system (10 Jinko 550W panels, 5kW Deye Inverter, 5.12kWh Lithium Battery) costs an all-inclusive price of approximately **95.5 million VND**, with a payback period of 4.5 years.
