# Solar Provider Survey Questions

Below is a list of 15 practical survey questions intended for rooftop solar installation and consulting companies:

**Group 1: Practical System Consulting**
1. For a house with an electricity bill of about 2 million VND/month, what system size (kWp) do you usually recommend?
2. What are the approximate costs for 3 kWp, 5 kWp, 6 kWp, and 10 kWp systems?
3. Approximately how many kWh/month does each system generate?
4. How many square meters of roof area does each system require?
5. What is the typical real-world payback period in years?

**Group 2: Technical Specifications**
6. What brands and models of solar panels are commonly used?
7. What is the wattage capacity of each panel (Wp)?
8. What is the efficiency percentage of the panels?
9. What brands of inverters are used, and what is their efficiency percentage?
10. What is the typical total system loss percentage in the real world, or what Performance Ratio (PR) is usually assumed?

**Group 3: Real-World Influencing Factors**
11. By what percentage do shading, roof orientation, and dust affect generation?
12. If a household uses mostly electricity at night, should they use battery storage?
13. By approximately how much does battery storage increase the system cost?
14. When consulting clients, what core information do you need to ask them?
15. Provide a sample quotation, a panel datasheet, or a real-world case study
